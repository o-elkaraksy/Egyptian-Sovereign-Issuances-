Methodology & coverage. Gross issuance is based on accepted auction amounts by issue date. Redemptions are accepted principal amounts by maturity date, including securities issued before the selected period. Weighted yields use accepted-amount weights and the CBE's weighted-average auction yield. Figures are face-value based and do not necessarily equal net cash raised. Zero-coupon auction records in the supplied file run through Mar-2026; Sukuk records run from Nov-2025 through Jun-2026. Asterisks identify instruments whose selected date range extends beyond the supplied coverage; their displayed totals are observed records only and may be incomplete. Oct-2026 is partial through 27-Oct-2026. Combined figures across instruments should be interpreted with these coverage limitations.
