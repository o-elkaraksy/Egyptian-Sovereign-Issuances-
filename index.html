Methodology & coverage. Gross issuance is based on accepted auction amounts by issue date. Redemptions are accepted principal amounts by maturity date, including securities issued before the selected period. Weighted yields use accepted-amount weights and the supplied CBE weighted-average auction yield. Maturity yield refers to the original auction yield of securities maturing in each month, not a secondary-market yield immediately before maturity. Forward maturities are contractual maturities already recorded in the source database; they are not forecasts of future issuance. Zero-coupon and Sukuk coverage is incomplete beyond their latest supplied auction records.
Source: Central Bank of Egypt · EGP bn unless otherwise stated · Live data fetched from the GitHub CSV source.
